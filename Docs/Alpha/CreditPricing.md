# Credit pricing baseline

1,000 credits represents US$1 of purchased credits. Credits are also earned by gameplay; ships and equipment are not cash-only. There is no redemption or cash payment implementation in this pass.

Hull and component base costs were reduced to one tenth of the previous values, retaining class relationships, component grade multipliers, rank and empire gates. Falcon and Stormhawk now cost 57,400 cr, Zodiac 45,900 cr, Prospector 46,300 cr and Crew Shuttle 45,000 cr. Authored mission base rewards, cash bonuses, promotion grants and medal grants were reduced by the same factor. Repeatable dynamic jobs retain their already modest 1,200–12,000 cr base rewards (before agent-level scaling). New careers now start at 5,000 cr; existing player wallets are never reduced or topped up. Training T1/T2 now pay 25,000/30,000 cr plus 1,200 cr recovery each. With the 5,000 cr starting balance, safe completion leaves exactly 5,000 cr after buying a 57,400 cr Stormhawk, without a cash purchase. Promotion points and rank gates are unchanged. No Alpha/Omega subscription gates are introduced. Prices are authored in ships.json and sys.json; no active saves are edited.

Skins retain category-flat prices: Normal 5,000 cr, Special 15,000 cr, Event 25,000 cr ($5/$15/$25 equivalents). EVE-inspired relative scarcity and quality relationships inform balance; its fluctuating PLEX/ISK exchange rate is not copied.

Existing owned hull valuations and insurance policies remain historical saved values. New hull purchases use current catalog pricing. Further tuning needs gameplay earning-rate measurements.
