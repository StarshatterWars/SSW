Mining and production
- Mining drones have their own model and beam. Repeat Mining sends a drone back
  to the same deposit after it returns and unloads; it stops when the deposit
  runs out or your hold is full. Lost, recalled and out-of-range drones stop.
- The Prospector carries three drones and the Rockhopper carries ten. Spare
  drones come from cargo, with drone cargo entries, blueprints and recipes.
- Drone production is assigned across twenty factory systems. Manufacturing
  checks recipe skills and supports additional recipes at a facility.
- Processing times and drone yields have been recalibrated alongside five
  commodity prices, 46 hull prices and component and weapon quality grades.
  Higher-grade equipment trades stronger performance for increased power use;
  grade III keeps its normal physical baseline. Prices remain SSW credits.

Station services and contracts
- A floating Station Services panel lets you inspect a station or carrier in
  flight. Its actions work only while docked at that exact facility. Carriers
  provide basic support; full component refits require a shipyard.
- Courier contracts offer a confirmed abandon action. Contract wallet entries
  identify individual operations more clearly. Contract IDs, stock ownership
  and inventory handoffs receive additional consistency checks.
- Guild listings follow your pilot's empire.

Data and launcher
- Awards, ranks, campaign catalogs, actions and zones move to editable JSON,
  with comparisons against their previous definitions to preserve progression.
- The launcher bundles its own hidden local Apache update server. Local test
  installs no longer need XAMPP. Game downloads remain separate from the
  launcher installer, and signed version and patch checks still apply.
